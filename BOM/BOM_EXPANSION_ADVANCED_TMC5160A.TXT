VARIANT: ADVANCED + TMC5160A - the most complete of the 4 variants:
         everything in BOM_EXPANSION_ADVANCED_TMC2209.TXT (slave MCU,
         both local sensor chips, local PWM support), but with the
         higher-power TMC5160A driver and its required external MOSFET
         power stage (see BOM_EXPANSION_BASIC_TMC5160A.TXT's own note on
         why that driver needs 8 external MOSFETs where TMC2209 needs
         none).
SOURCE: Same provisional-BOM caveat as every other expansion-board BOM
        in this set - no schematic exists yet.
